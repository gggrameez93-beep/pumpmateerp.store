UNIFIED 4-IN-1 ACCOUNTS MODULE

One Ledger System for Every Entity

Instead of scattered modules, v3.1 unifies four account types inside the Accounts module. Every account can be used directly in Daily Entry Section B and viewed in the Unified Ledger:

1. Customer (e.g. CMGM, SURGLAB, FAUJI): Debit = Credit fuel sale (Dr Accounts Receivable / Cr Sales Revenue). Credit = Payment received. Supports multiple vehicles & credit limits.
2. Expense Head (e.g. EXOV, EXGEN, EXMISC): Debit in Section B = Posts Dr <Head Name> Expense / Cr Cash. Gives each recurring expense category its own running ledger.
3. Employee (e.g. EMP1, ERA, MSK): Debit in Section B or Employees tab = Dr Salary Expense / Cr Cash. Credit = Salary recovery/deduction.
4. Income Head (e.g. MP — Month Profit): Credit in Section B = Dr Cash / Cr MP. Accumulates operating profit and extra station income.
THE MP (MONTH PROFIT) ENGINE

How the 3 Profit & Income Routes Add Together

PumpMate ERP v3.1 introduces the MP (Month Profit) Income Head account. Money flows into MP through three distinct, non-overlapping routes:

ROUTE 1 · Automatic Month-End Operating Profit

Go to Reports → 🗓 Post Month Profit. Calculates Total Fuel/Lube Sales Revenue − Total Expenses for the date range and posts Dr Sales Revenue / Cr MP.

ROUTE 2 · Manual Credit in Daily Entry Section B

Select Party MP in Section B and enter a Credit amount for ad-hoc rebates, supplier bonuses, or commissions (Dr Cash / Cr MP).

ROUTE 3 · "Other Income" in Expenses Module

In Expenses → New Entry → Type: Income (Credit) (e.g. car wash, shop rent, air/scrap sale). Automatically posts Dr Cash or Bank / Cr MP.
