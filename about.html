THE STORY & PURPOSE BEHIND PUMPMATE ERP

Why Did We Build PumpMate ERP? (Detailed Industry Analysis)

Before building PumpMate ERP, I spent months studying how petrol pumps and fuel stations (PSO, Shell, Attock, Hascol, Total Parco) operate on the ground in Pakistan. Station owners were losing hundreds of thousands of rupees every year due to six critical operational problems that neither paper registers nor generic accounting software could solve:

01

Manual 12-Nozzle Register Math Errors

In a traditional PSO paper register, the munshi manually copies yesterday's closing meter readings as today's opening readings for 12 nozzles (4 Diesel, 6 Super, 2 HOBC). A single digit transposition or calculator mistake of just 20 litres costs the station over Rs 7,000 in unrecorded cash.

✓ Our Solution: Day 1 is the only day you type Opening readings. From Day 2 forever, yesterday's locked Closing automatically becomes today's Opening.
02

Fortnightly OGRA / PSO Fuel Price Revisions

Every 15 days in Pakistan, fuel prices change. When a tanker was bought at Rs 370/L and a new tanker arrives at Rs 375/L, paper registers and basic POS tools fail to separate old-cost stock from new-cost stock, distorting Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) and actual tank valuation.

✓ Our Solution: True FIFO (First-In, First-Out) Wet Stock layer tracking with automatic Price History logging and unsold stock revaluation.
03

Credit Customer (Udhaar) & Vehicle Slip Leaks

Transport companies and factories send multiple vehicles (trucks, tractors, vans) to refuel on credit slips throughout the month. At month-end, compiling bills by vehicle number and rate period from paper khata books takes days and often misses slips.

✓ Our Solution: 1-Click SKP-standard Customer Billing in 3 formats (Rate-Grouped Invoice, Detailed Statement, and Vehicle-Wise Summary).
04

Internet Downtime & Cloud Subscription Traps

Many petrol pumps are located on GT Roads, highways, and rural routes where internet connections frequently drop. Cloud SaaS software stops working during internet outages and charges Rs 5,000 to Rs 15,000 every single month forever.

✓ Our Solution: 100% Offline browser architecture. Zero internet needed for daily work, zero server maintenance, and zero monthly rent.
05

Disconnected Ledgers & Missing Month Profit

Station owners needed a single place to see Customers, Recurring Expense Heads (like Generator Diesel or Owner Vehicle), Staff Salaries, and Other Income (service bay, tuck shop rent) without hiring a chartered accountant.

✓ Our Solution: A 4-in-1 Unified Accounts system and 3-Route MP (Month Profit) engine that generates Trial Balance, P&L, and Balance Sheet automatically.
06

Financial Privacy & Tamper Prevention

Station owners do not want their sensitive daily sales and profit figures stored on someone else's cloud server, nor do they want past locked days altered silently without audit recalculation.

✓ Our Solution: Local encrypted database storage on your own station PC, Day-Lock protection, and hardware-bound site licensing.
